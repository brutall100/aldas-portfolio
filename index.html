01 — About

A little about me

From cutting wood with CNC machines to cutting code.

Curiosity first

Programming is the hobby that became my work. Almost 30 projects so far, from a first dice game to a four-language shop that takes real payments, and each one taught me something new.

Front to back

I build the whole thing: React, Next.js or Preact on the screen; Node.js, Deno, Express or PHP behind it; PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MySQL or SQLite for the data.

AI and real time

I like apps that answer back: weekly meal plans from the Claude API, book tips from OpenAI, chats that run live on Socket.IO or straight between browsers with WebRTC.

Finished, not just started

Every project gets a live demo, a clear README, light and dark themes and a layout that works on a phone. The bigger ones run tests and checks in CI before they ship.

Precision background

Eighteen years as a CNC operator taught me to read specs carefully, debug patiently and respect deadlines. The same habits carry straight into code.

Off the keyboard

You'll find me reading, fishing, out in nature, or deep into a good series. Rest is part of the process.
